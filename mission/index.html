Mission

Professional tools, available everywhere.

Good developer tools should not depend on the computer you happen to be sitting at.

01Principles

Open the door
No installer, no licence key, no required account. If you have a modern browser, you have an IDE.
Keep it yours
Your files live in your browser. We ship no telemetry. Your keys and tokens stay on your device.
Be plain about limits
We say what runs in a browser and what needs a server, and we label what is not finished.
Build on open work
The editor, the interpreter, the registry and the type are open source. We contribute what we can back.
Make it readable
Interfaces and themes target WCAG AA contrast and work from the keyboard.

02Where we are heading

  • Real language servers running in web workers, starting with the languages people ask for most.
  • A remote runner so builds and terminals work from any browser.
  • Better offline behaviour.

The roadmap says what is finished and what is not.
