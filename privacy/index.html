Privacy

What stays in your browser and what leaves it.

Short, specific and checkable in the source.

01Stays on your device

  • Your files and workspace, in the browser's IndexedDB.
  • Settings, keybindings, and which workspace you opened last.
  • Your GitHub token and any AI API key.

02Leaves your browser, only when you use it

DestinationWhenWhat
github.comWhen you sign in or open a repositoryYour sign-in, repository reads, and the commits you make.
Your AI providerWhen you chat or accept completionsYour prompt and the code you share.
This siteWhen you load itThe app files, and the extension catalog you browse.

03What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party scripts.
  • Fonts and the Python runtime are served from this site.
  • The only cookie is a short-lived sign-in state cookie during GitHub sign-in.

04Clearing your data

Sign out from the status bar to remove your GitHub token. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything else the IDE stored.
